Brand Philosophy

We believe the most sustainable garment is the one that already exists. We give old clothes a second life instead of producing new ones.

Upcycling Process

  1. Collect unwanted clothes from the community.
  2. Sort and wash by fabric type.
  3. Deconstruct and redesign into new patterns.
  4. Sew, quality-check, and ship.

Eco Manifesto
